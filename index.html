Allergen Guard

For millions of people, every grocery trip is a gamble. Tiny text. Foreign languages. Hidden derivatives. A single overlooked ingredient can trigger a life-threatening reaction.

Parents read every label twice. Teenagers avoid eating out. Travellers carry laminated allergy cards in four languages. It shouldn't be this hard to eat safely.

Allergen Guard was born from this exact frustration — a personal experience turned into a mission to make food safety instant, intelligent, and accessible to everyone.

Core Features

Four ways to protect you.

From barcode scanning to emergency calls — every feature designed for when it matters most.

Instant Barcode Scan

Point your camera at any barcode. Product data is fetched instantly from OpenFoodFacts — name, brand, ingredients, allergens, and traces. Know what you're eating in under 3 seconds.

Real-time 98.2% accuracy Auto-detect

AI Label Reading

No barcode? Snap a photo of any ingredient label. Google Gemini 2.5 Flash analyzes the text, extracts every ingredient, and cross-references your allergy profile. Works with handwritten menus too.

Gemini AI Photo analysis 94.7% recall

Smart Allergen Matching

500+ synonym keywords across 5 languages. "Kasein" maps to milk. "Lécithine" maps to soy. Works offline, catches derivatives human eyes miss, and supports 16 major allergen categories.

5 languages Offline 500+ synonyms

Siri Emergency Call

During an allergic reaction, every second counts — and your hands may be occupied with an EpiPen. Say "Hey Siri, Allergen Emergency" to instantly call your preset emergency contact. Completely hands-free. Potentially life-saving.

Hands-free Siri Integration Life-saving

How It Works

Three steps to certainty.

From scan to verdict in under 3 seconds. No account required.

Scan

Point your camera at a barcode or ingredient label. The app detects it automatically — no button press needed.

AI Analyzes

Gemini AI reads ingredients, matches against your allergen profile across 500+ synonyms in 5 languages.

Get Your Verdict

Clear traffic-light result: Safe, Caution, or Avoid — with confidence scores and detailed allergen breakdown.
